Participant feedback

Themes from the post-workshop usability survey, summarized from open-text responses.

Participants reported feeling noticeably less wary of AI once they understood its supportive, non-diagnostic role in areas like radiology.

Theme from open-text survey responses

Several responses credited the workshop with reducing their instinct to self-diagnose online, in favor of consulting a qualified professional.

Theme from open-text survey responses

The F.A.S.T. stroke check and the live quiz were the two most-mentioned highlights, especially among older participants.

Theme from open-text survey responses

Interactive formats sparked peer discussion on blood sugar checks, hydration and hygiene that continued after sessions ended.
